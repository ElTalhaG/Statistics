# Lab 9 - Chapter 6 Exercises

This folder contains worked solutions for Exercises 6.1 and 6.2 in [Lab 9 Answers.md](<Lab 9 Answers.md>).

Run the analysis and generate diagnostic/interval plots with:

    python3 lab_9_multiple_regression.py

The script fits the nitrate model and the full/reduced models for Exercise 6.2, prints regression output, and saves the plots beside itself. Dependencies: NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, SciPy, and statsmodels.

Source PDF: [Chapter 6 exercise solutions](<statistics_exercises_ch6_solution.pdf>); exercise statements: [DTU Chapter 6 PDF](https://02323.compute.dtu.dk/enotes/exercises6-Python.pdf).
